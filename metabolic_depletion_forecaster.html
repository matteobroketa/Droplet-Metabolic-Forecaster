Model scope

Growth responds to O₂, nutrient, lactate, and pH stress but does not model cell death. Carbonate/alkalinity chemistry uses a linear non-bicarbonate buffer rather than a complete explicit-medium charge-balance model.

How this result was calculated

Settings are saved in the browser. No data leaves the page.

How to use this forecaster

  1. Describe the experiment. Choose the cell line and medium, then enter the productive-droplet volume and cells in that droplet.
  2. Describe the physical setup. Set emulsion volume, aqueous fraction, oil reservoir, vessel, and whether gas is replenished or sealed.
  3. Calculate and read the first limit. The main result is the first configured viability endpoint; the four endpoint values explain why it occurred.
  4. Plan with deterministic cases. Compare high-demand, nominal, and low-demand cases. They are scenario cases, not a confidence interval.
  5. Act on cautions. Only current setup cautions appear with the result. Open Model Scope and Diagnostics for assumptions, transport details, and raw time series.
Model structure. Oxygen is integrated as coupled compartments: evaluated droplet, bulk droplet population, emulsion oil, residual oil, and gas phase. Glucose and glutamine are evaluated inside the productive droplet. pH can use a carbonate/alkalinity solver with DIC, bicarbonate, carbonate, water, and linear non-bicarbonate buffer alkalinity, or the legacy Henderson-Hasselbalch approximation for backward comparison.

Equations and calculation model

Concentration units. Oxygen is represented in aqueous-equivalent μM. For a droplet volume in nL, 1 μM corresponds to 1 fmol/nL. Glucose, glutamine, lactate, bicarbonate, and CO₂ are tracked in mM; 1 fmol/nL equals 0.001 mM.
Droplet geometry and population

The droplet volume slider is logarithmic.

V_nL = 0.07 × 10^(2t), 0 ≤ t ≤ 1
V_μm³ = V_nL × 10⁶
r = (3V / 4π)^(1/3)
A = 4πr²

The emulsion population is calculated from total emulsion volume and aqueous fraction. Bulk growth uses Poisson occupancy classes so empty droplets stay empty.

V_emulsion,μL = 1000 × V_emulsion,mL
V_aq,μL = V_emulsion,μL × aqueous_fraction
V_oil,emulsion,μL = V_emulsion,μL − V_aq,μL
V_oil,total,μL = V_oil,emulsion,μL + V_reservoir,μL
V_liquid,μL = V_emulsion,μL + V_reservoir,μL = V_aq,μL + V_oil,total,μL
N_droplets = V_aq,μL × 1000 / V_droplet,nL
P_k = exp(−λ) λ^k / k!
E[cells | target K] = K + λ(N − 1)
Oil, reservoir, and headspace capacities

Carrier oil is represented as an oxygen reservoir in water-equivalent concentration units. This preserves Henry-law concentration gradients while allowing fluorinated oil to hold much more oxygen than water.

Capacity_target = V_droplet,nL
Capacity_bulk_aqueous = max(0, V_aq,nL − V_droplet,nL)
Capacity_emulsion_oil = V_emulsion_oil,nL × oil_capacity_ratio
Capacity_reservoir_oil = V_residual_oil,nL × oil_capacity_ratio
nO₂_headspace = pO₂ × V_headspace / (RT)
Henry-law gas boundary

The gas boundary determines equilibrium oxygen at exposed oil or aqueous surfaces. Initial liquid and headspace oxygen are independent user inputs.

pO₂ = xO₂ × (1 − pH₂O(T))
[O₂]eq = K_H,O₂(37 °C) × exp(−0.025(T−37)) × pO₂ × 1000
initial_liquid_O₂ = [O₂]eq(air) × air_saturation_% / 100
nO₂_headspace,0 = xO₂,initial × (1 − pH₂O) × V_headspace / (RT)
custom gas validity: O₂_% + CO₂_% ≤ 100; balance gas = 100 − O₂_% − CO₂_%
headspace_mL = (vessel_capacity_µL − generated_emulsion_µL − excess_oil_µL)/1000; if negative, configuration is invalid
tubing_capacity_µL = π(d/2)²L
filled_length_mm = min(L, liquid_fill_µL / (π(d/2)²)); tubing_wall_area_mm² = πd × filled_length
O₂_threshold = user-selected mode: selected-gas %, air %, or absolute µM
Mass-transfer network

The model stores conserved O₂ amounts and derives concentrations from them. Droplet transfer scales with droplet radius through an A/V term.

k = ln(2) / half_time
k_drop ∝ (V_ref / V_drop)^(1/3)
F_target = k_drop,target × C_target × (O₂_oil − O₂_target)
F_bulk = k_drop,bulk × C_bulk × (O₂_oil − O₂_bulk)
F_oil = k_oil × C_emulsion_oil × (O₂_reservoir − O₂_emulsion_oil)
F_gas,res = k_gas,res × C_reservoir_oil × (O₂_eq(headspace) − O₂_reservoir)
F_gas,direct = k_gas,direct × C_emulsion_oil × surface_access × (O₂_eq(headspace) − O₂_emulsion_oil)

Geometry-corrected mode scales half-times by vessel area, emulsion depth, residual oil depth, storage mode, and exposed surface. Ideal mode is retained only as a comparison.

Metabolic rate inputs

Default and user-entered rates are used directly as condition-matched values in fmol/cell/min. The model applies no universal temperature, serum, additive, Warburg, or inhibitor multiplier. Temperature still affects gas solubility and carbonate chemistry.

OCR_eff = OCR_input
GCR_eff = GCR_input
LPR_eff = LPR_input
GlnCR_eff = GlnCR_input

Below the Pasteur threshold, glycolysis and lactate production increase smoothly up to the selected maximum multiplier.

Substrate and pH integration

Carbonate / alkalinity pH mode. The default pH layer treats aqueous tracked carbon as dissolved inorganic carbon (DIC) and solves carbonate speciation from total alkalinity, water dissociation, and linear non-bicarbonate buffer capacity.

Consumption and production are integrated with adaptive sub-steps to avoid unstable fast-exchange updates. For bicarbonate-buffered media, the initial dissolved CO₂ is inferred from starting pH and bicarbonate. The pH boundary mode then chooses whether the boundary is held at starting pH, driven by selected-gas CO₂, or governed by finite closed-headspace CO₂ mass balance.

dO₂_target/dt = F_target − n_target × OCR_eff
dGlucose/dt = −n_target × GCR_eff / V_droplet,nL / 1000
dGlutamine/dt = −n_target × GlnCR_eff / V_droplet,nL / 1000
dLactate/dt = n_target × LPR_eff / V_droplet,nL / 1000
DIC = [CO₂*] + [HCO₃⁻] + [CO₃²⁻]
TA(t) = TA₀ − Δlactate + β_nonbicarb × (pH − pH₀)
fixed boundary: CO₂_boundary = CO₂_initial
selected gas boundary: CO₂_boundary = K_H,CO₂ × xCO₂ × (1 − pH₂O)
closed headspace: CO₂_boundary(t) is derived from headspace CO₂ mass via PV = nRT
dDIC/dt = n_target × OCR_eff × RQ / V_droplet,nL / 1000 − exchange_relief
solve TA = [HCO₃⁻] + 2[CO₃²⁻] + [OH⁻] − [H⁺] + β_nonbicarb × (pH − pH₀)
legacy mode: pH = pH₀ + (pKₐ + log10([HCO₃⁻](t)/CO₂(t)) − pH_baseline)
Proliferation and endpoint

When proliferation is enabled, target-cell number follows logistic growth with the configured carrying capacity.

K = carrying_capacity_cells_per_nL × V_droplet,nL
n(t) = K n₀ exp(rt) / (K + n₀(exp(rt) − 1))
r = ln(2) / doubling_time

First configured threshold crossing. The displayed time is the first crossing of target-droplet oxygen threshold, glucose minimum, glutamine minimum, pH floor, or pH ceiling. It is not a survival or function endpoint unless prospectively linked to one. If no crossing occurs within the configured horizon, the result is displayed as ≥ horizon.
